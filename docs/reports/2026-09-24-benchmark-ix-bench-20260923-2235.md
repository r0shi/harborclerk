## Reading

**This is the second half of the re-baseline: Gemma 4 26B-A4B and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on CUAD, on the build that carries #698, #699, #700 and #701 (`67dd3e5`), against the same questions, the same judge and the same Claude baselines as `bench-20260922-2305` (#702).** Both models ran all 16 units. No unit errored, the model server logged no Metal out-of-memory and the API signalled no restart, and every research fan-out finished inside the reaper's five minutes. With the first half, all four curated models on this machine now have a measured row on a healthy index under the current judge.

### How this run relates to the first half

- **The baselines are copied, not re-bought.** The 16 Claude answers in `baselines/cuad/` are the files `bench-20260922-2305` produced on 2026-09-23 (USD 7.98 then; USD 0 here). They were made through the same index and the same search code apart from the two reranker changes since (the four-pair batch and the NaN guard), neither of which changes what a search returns when the reranker is healthy. The judge compares each answer with that baseline, so the two halves are comparable with each other and with nothing before them.
- **The preflight was run after the sweep, not before.** The operator (this agent) skipped the skill's first step. The check above was taken at rest at 04:50Z, the model deactivated, and says: thermal nominal, on AC, GPU idle, `llama-server` at the pin, both models fit, 78% free, 3.3 GB of swap. The swap is the one figure a pre-run check would have read the same. Nothing in it says the machine was unfit during the run, and nothing in it is evidence about the run's first minute either.
- The context each model ran at is what #698 gives a 32 GB Mac: Gemma **177,152** (262,144 before), Qwen3.6 **16,384** (73,728 before, which OOMed under research). That is the price of the fix, and this run is what it buys.

### What the numbers support

- **Gemma 4 26B-A4B is the first model on this machine to do well at research.** 4 of its 6 research answers scored 4 or 5 on answers-question (the other two: one 3, one 0), with a citation overlap of 0.95 against the baseline; in the first half no model that finished research scored above 3 on any research question (gpt-oss-20b 0 of 6, qwen3-8b 0 of 6). Its `ask` results are the weak side: 3 of 10 at 4 or 5, seven at 0–1, and a median of 78 s with one 554 s outlier.
- **Qwen3.6 at 16,384 tokens is a capable `ask` model and a poor research one.** 4 of 10 `ask` answers at 4 or 5 (the same four questions it passed in the first half, at 73,728), median 66 s, none over 134 s. Research: 1 of 6 at 4 or 5, three at 0. A 16K context is a research handicap by construction (the notes prompt is capped at what fits), and this is what a 32 GB Mac gets from this model now; the models page says so.
- Judge cost for 32 verdicts: USD 0.05. The run cost nothing else.
