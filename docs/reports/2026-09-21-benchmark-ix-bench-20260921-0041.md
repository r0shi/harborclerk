## Reading

**This run is not a baseline, and no number in the tables above should be compared with anything.** It was meant to re-baseline the current models after the llama.cpp `v0.4.1` bump (#549). It was the first metered, end-to-end run of the harness on the mini after the GPU throttle was cleared (#652), and what it measured was mostly the product and the harness failing in ways neither reported. It is published because those failures are the result.

### Why the numbers do not count

- **Retrieval was broken for the whole run (#685).** Six minutes into ingest the embedder's MPS cache went from 4 GB to 43 GB, its drain reclaimed nothing, and from 00:53 every `/embed` call returned 500. The worker logged 117 successful embeds and 249 failures, so most of the 80 CUAD documents were indexed without vectors; every query-time embed failed and search fell back to lexical-only, silently. Every model, **and the Claude baseline they are scored against**, searched that degraded index.
- **Research found nothing at all.** For "Compare termination notice periods across the corpus", Research planned 15 queries and recorded "Found 0 unique passages across 0 documents", on a corpus of commercial contracts. Three different models then reported that the corpus does not discuss the topic. The six research questions score near zero for every model for that reason, not because of the models.
- **`qwen36-35b-a3b` was never measured.** Its server returned Metal "Insufficient Memory" on the first prompt at the app's chosen context of 241,664 (#684). The app answered every question in 0.0 to 0.1 s with a canned sentence and `status: completed`; the harness recorded ten of them as `done` and paid to have them judged. Its row is an artefact. Whether the memory budget or the embedder's 43 GB caused the failure is not established.
- **The preflight's `warn` understated the machine.** It saw 2.8 GB of swap before the run. By the end the machine had 58.8 GB of swap in use, because of the embedder. The preflight checks the embedder's `/health`, which said 200 throughout.

### What can be said

- Thermal pressure read Nominal at every check, and decode through the app was unthrottled (Gemma 4 26B-A4B at a steady 16.8 tok/s with the GPU at 99%), so #652 stays closed.
- The three models that ran did run on the pinned llama.cpp through the signed app, and completed all 16 units each. One `qwen3-8b` unit degraded by emitting tool-call syntax as text.
- The judge's verdict is relative to the Claude baseline's completeness, not a measure of correctness. `gpt-oss-20b` named both parties to an agreement correctly, with full citation overlap, and was judged "marginal, 3 of 5" because the baseline also gave their addresses. So "1 pass in 16" is not "6% correct", and would not be even on a healthy index. That is a property of the harness to keep in mind for the real baseline, and an argument for #661 and #663.

### What the run cost, and why

USD 19.58 of the 25.00 cap, against a pre-run estimate of USD 2.64 that I relayed to the owner before starting. USD 18.80 of it was the 16 Claude baseline questions: about 376,000 input tokens each against an estimate of 20,000, because the baseline is an unbounded tool loop that re-sends its growing transcript with no cache breakpoints. One question took 24 calls and about USD 6 (#682). If the other three corpora cost anything like CUAD, a four-corpus sweep does not fit under the cap; they were not measured.

### What stopped the run, and what was done about it

1. 30 minutes in, the MCP session's token expired and the sweep exited on an uncaught exception (#681). Resumed; same run, same ledger.
2. The first local-model unit crashed because spaCy's `en_core_web_sm` was not installed in the harness environment (#683). Installed the way CI does; resumed.
3. That crash left `gemma4-26b-a4b / cuad-ask-1` marked `done` with no metrics row, because status is saved before metrics are computed (#683). That one unit was re-run with `--rerun` after the sweep completed: not to improve a number, none had been recorded.
4. During that re-run `GET /api/chat/models/status` returned 500 while the app swapped model servers (it catches connection refusals and timeouts, not resets). Resumed once the model was up.

The suite commit and the clean tree were the same for every start.

### Not verified

- Which documents ended up without vectors, and whether the pipeline marks them for retry.
- Why the embedder's drain reclaimed nothing (the installed app bundles torch 2.11.0; `embedder/uv.lock` pins 2.13.0).
- Whether `qwen36-35b-a3b` loads at its budgeted context on a healthy machine.
- `qwen3-4b`, the Qwen3.5 pair and Gemma 4 12B are not downloaded on this machine and were not run.

### Before the real baseline

#685 first: nothing measured through a failing embedder means anything. Then a preflight that probes `POST /embed` and a post-ingest check that the chunks have vectors, a per-model canary (#684), and a bounded, cached baseline loop (#682) so the run costs what it says it will. #549 stays open.
