## Reading

**This is the re-baseline after #691 and #692, on a healthy index, and it is a baseline for two of the four models.** `gpt-oss-20b` and `qwen3-8b` ran all 16 units each against a Claude baseline that itself searched a working index, judged by `gpt-5.6-luna` on the rubric from #695. `gemma4-26b-a4b` and `qwen36-35b-a3b` did not get a fair run, for one reason, and their rows should not be compared with anything.

### What made the run stop

The machine paged. The memory budget's 6 GB host headroom was measured at idle, and under search the reranker holds 6.9 GB and the embedder 3.0 GB. Gemma at 262,144 tokens and Qwen3.6 at 73,728 each need about 25 GB, so with the two services the GPU was asked for 35 GB against Metal's 28.1 GB working set (#698). Measured from the API log, every search took 3.3 to 4.1 s with `gpt-oss-20b` or `qwen3-8b` resident and a median of 9 s, with tails to 30 s, under the other two. Research runs up to 35 searches with no heartbeat or SSE event between them (#700), so at that speed the app's five-minute reaper and the harness's 325 s watchdog killed tasks that were still working: all five remaining Gemma research units and Qwen3.6's first. Each abort then cost the harness a 33-minute read timeout, twice per unit (#701); that is the five hours between 00:25 and 06:00. The sweep was stopped by hand at 12:07, resumed with Qwen3.6's six research units skipped by the operator, and closed with nothing pending.

Two more faults surfaced in the same windows. Qwen3.6 hit Metal "Insufficient Memory" at the #692 context on a 30k-character research prompt (recorded on #684): the hand loads that verified 73,728 were made with the services idle. And the reranker returned a NaN score once, which pydantic serialised as `null` and which crashed `hybrid_search`'s conflict detection, losing a batch of five queries (#699).

### What the numbers support

- **`gpt-oss-20b` against `qwen3-8b`, on CUAD, 16 questions each, one judge.** gpt-oss answered 5 of 10 `ask` questions at answers-question 4 or 5; qwen3-8b answered 2 of 10. gpt-oss's citation overlap with the baseline was 0.76 to qwen3-8b's 0.62. qwen3-8b was three and a half times faster on `ask` (median 91 s to 310 s) and its worst unit was 167 s where gpt-oss's was 2,088 s: one `ask` in which gpt-oss made 20 model calls and 17 searches over 35 minutes, with no error, and was judged 0.
- **Research is weak for every model that finished it.** Across the 13 research units judged (gpt-oss 6, qwen3-8b 6, Gemma 1), no answer scored 4 or 5 on answers-question; the best was 3. Research latencies ran 361 to 3,050 s, the two over 2,500 s being units the harness aborted once and passed on retry (the same fault as above, on the borderline `gpt-oss-20b` and once on `qwen3-8b`). The baseline the judge compares against is a cloud model with the same tools, so this is a statement about the local research pipeline on this hardware, not only about the models.
- **The bounded, cached baseline (#682) cost USD 7.98 for 16 questions**, against USD 18.80 last run: 83 calls, 3.77 M cache-read and 1.02 M cache-write tokens, 48 K output tokens. The spend table above shows only 115 uncached input tokens because it does not yet print the cache columns the ledger records; the ledger does.
- **The judge cost USD 0.07 for 53 verdicts.** With `gpt-5.6-luna` the judge is no longer a line item.

### What the numbers do not support

- Anything about `gemma4-26b-a4b`. Its ten `ask` units ran under the paging described above (latencies 14 s to 824 s; the same questions took qwen3-8b 74 to 167 s), five of its six research units errored, and the one that passed spent 310 s in its search phase, 15 s under the watchdog.
- Anything about `qwen36-35b-a3b`. Its row is ten `ask` units. Two of them, `ask-8` and `ask-9`, are artefacts: the server had just OOMed, the app answered in 0.3 s and 0.1 s with its canned "wasn't able to formulate a complete response" and `status: completed`, and the harness judged them (#684 item 3, still open). The other eight ran on the configuration that paged in its research window; whether they paged too was not measured. Its six research units were skipped and are counted in `planned` but not `ran`.
- Comparison with `bench-20260921-0041`: that run's index had no vectors (#685) and its judge and rubric were different (#694, #695). Nothing here is a delta from it.
- Comparison of the two sound rows beyond CUAD: one corpus, 16 questions, one judge. The other three corpora have not been run under the new judge.

### Preflight

`warn` for 6 GB of swap in use before the run began, on a machine at 76% free; everything else passed, including "fits in 32 GiB" for all four models. That check is the budget's own arithmetic and it is what #698 says is wrong: a fit check that does not count the embedder and reranker under load passes exactly the two models that then paged.
