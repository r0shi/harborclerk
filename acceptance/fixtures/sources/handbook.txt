STAFF HANDBOOK

Quill & Tern Bookbinders Ltd

1. Working hours
The workshop is open from 08:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday. Staff are
entitled to a one hour unpaid lunch break.

2. Holiday
Full time staff receive 25 days of paid holiday per year in addition to
public holidays. Requests should be made to Tomas Reyes at least two
weeks in advance.

3. Health and safety
The guillotine and board chopper may only be operated by staff who have
completed the induction. Guards must never be removed. Report any fault
to Priya Natarajan immediately.

4. Deliveries
Pallets delivered to the Harbourside Lane loading bay must be moved
inside on the morning of delivery.
