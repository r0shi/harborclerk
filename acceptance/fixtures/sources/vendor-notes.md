# Vendor notes

Working notes on suppliers for the Harbourside Lane workshop. Updated
monthly by Priya Natarajan.

## Bindery supplies

Northgate Paper Co. supplies the archival board and book cloth. Their
account manager is Dev Okafor. Standard lead time is ten working days;
rush orders carry a 15 percent surcharge.

## Harbourside Lane deliveries

Deliveries arrive at the rear loading bay between 08:00 and 10:00. The
bay is shared with the ground floor tenant next door, so pallets must be
moved inside the same morning.

## Equipment servicing

The guillotine is serviced by Severn Machine Care every six months. Last
service: 9 April 2025. Next due: October 2025.
