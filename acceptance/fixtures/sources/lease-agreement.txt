COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement (the "Lease") is made on 14 February 2025
between Marlowe Holdings Ltd, a company registered in England under number
08812345 whose registered office is at 3 Quayside Court, Bristol (the
"Landlord"), and Quill & Tern Bookbinders Ltd, whose registered office is at
14 Harbourside Lane, Bristol (the "Tenant").

1. PREMISES
The Landlord lets to the Tenant the ground floor workshop and first floor
office at 14 Harbourside Lane, Bristol, together with the loading bay to the
rear (the "Premises").

2. TERM
The term of this Lease is three years commencing on 1 March 2025 and
expiring on 28 February 2028 (the "Term").

3. RENT
The Tenant shall pay rent of 2,400 pounds per calendar month, payable in
advance on the first working day of each month by bank transfer to the
account notified by the Landlord.

4. RENEWAL
The Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of three years by
giving written notice not less than six months before the expiry date. The
rent for the renewed term shall be agreed between the parties or, failing
agreement, determined by an independent surveyor appointed by the President
of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors.

5. INSURANCE
The Landlord shall insure the building. The Tenant shall insure its own
fixtures, stock and equipment and shall provide evidence of cover on request.

6. REPAIRS
The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good repair and shall
not make structural alterations without the Landlord's written consent.

7. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Lease on the second anniversary of the
commencement date by giving not less than six months' written notice.

Signed on behalf of the Landlord: Eleanor Marlowe, Director
Signed on behalf of the Tenant: Tomas Reyes, Managing Director
