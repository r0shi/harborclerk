1. **Terroir** is the influence of the land on the wine, including the soil, climate, and topography. The same grape grown in different regions can produce markedly different wines. A fine brush can work as well as a broad stroke, with examples of Grand Cru and Premier Cru Burgundy wines having different characters even when vineyards are close. [Terroir-6-dfs, page ?]
